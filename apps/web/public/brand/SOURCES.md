### Bildprompt

Create one wide cinematic website background, 16:9 landscape. Blend the THREE supplied photographs into an elegant soft photographic montage, not a fictitious single building: on the left the close-up IW nära window signage of photo 1, subtly layered with the feather flag and facade from photo 2; on the right the gym entrance with illuminated IW nära GYM sign from photo 3. Keep recognizable original logos and architectural elements. Use soft natural crossfades with no hard collage borders, leave center relatively quiet for white website text. VERY IMPORTANT: dark, cozy, muted neutral charcoal, black and warm subdued bronze lighting. Convert blue sky to charcoal gray, remove ALL blue/cyan and bright green color casts, mute orange to dark warm brown, white flag to dim silver gray. Evening low-key exposure, gentle warm lights from windows and sign, realistic photography. No added text or UI. This is a mood montage assembled from supplied photos, not a documentary photograph of a new facility. Deliver a single usable background asset.
